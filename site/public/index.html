Tour

A side rail, rows you can collapse, and a switch for movies, shows or both on every list. Every screenshot is the real app on a real Trakt account.

Title pages

Poster, overview and one play button that knows where you are, then seasons, cast, similar titles and lists. Movies also show their vibes: what viewers say about them, from Nanocrowd.

  • Mark watched now, on release day or on any date
  • Watchlist, favourites and your own Trakt lists
  • Trailers play inside the app

Player

The player bar sits above the video, never over it. Pick a season, skip to an episode, switch servers or press Next. Pop-ups and in-player ads are blocked.

  • Auto picks the sharpest server and remembers it for each title
  • Loads up to 3 minutes ahead on servers that allow it, so seeking doesn't wait
  • Near the end it offers to add the watch to Trakt; nothing is sent until you press it

Stats

Filmie keeps a copy of your Trakt history on your PC and works out your totals, top genres, ratings and monthly activity from it. No VIP needed, and it works offline.

  • Movies, shows, episodes and time watched
  • A daily streak on Home
  • Deleted when you sign out

Features

Browse what's on
Trending, popular, anticipated, box office and the weekly charts, plus a calendar of what's coming out.
Browse by vibe
Nanocrowd's genres with the words viewers use for them, and each movie's vibes as a word cloud.
Sync with Trakt
Up next, continue watching, watchlist, lists, history and recommendations, kept in step in the background.
Marked only when you say
No check-ins and no scrobbling. Near the end of a video Filmie asks; your history changes only when you press it.
Bring your own servers
Filmie ships with none. Add the servers you have the right to use; Auto picks the sharpest and remembers it for each title.
Fast and offline-ready
Pages come from a local cache first, so they open instantly and still work when the network doesn't.
Private by default
No accounts, analytics or telemetry of its own. Your data stays on your PC and with services you choose.
Free and open source
MIT licensed, with the code and every release on GitHub.

Get started

  1. Install

    Download the installer and run it. It adds Microsoft's WebView2 runtime if your PC needs it.

  2. Connect Trakt

    Sign in with a short code to bring in your library. It's optional, so you can browse without an account.

  3. Add a server

    Filmie includes none. Add yours in Settings with its link and how its addresses are built.

FAQ

Does Filmie include movies or TV shows?

No. Filmie doesn't host or supply video and includes no video servers. It shows information from TMDB, Trakt and Nanocrowd and can open servers you add yourself. You're responsible for making sure you have the right to use them.

Is it free?

Yes. Filmie is free and open source under the MIT License.

Do I need a Trakt account?

No, you can browse without one. You need a free Trakt account for your library, recommendations, stats and watch tracking. Trakt VIP isn't needed.

Why does Windows show a warning when I install it?

The installer isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may warn you. To continue, choose More info, then Run anyway. Each release lists a SHA-256 checksum you can verify first.

Does it work on Mac or Linux?

Not at the moment. Filmie runs on 64-bit Windows 10 and 11.

Are the screenshots real?

Yes. They're taken from the app itself, signed in to the developer's own Trakt account, playing from a server the developer added. Filmie itself ships with no video servers.
