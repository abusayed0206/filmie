Every movie and show you love. Tracked on your terms.

Filmie is a small Windows app for browsing what's on, keeping your Trakt history in step and playing from servers you add yourself. Nothing is marked as watched until you say so.

Take the tour

Built for the way you watch

A side rail, rows you can collapse, and a switch for movies, shows or both on every list. Every screenshot here is the real app on a real Trakt account.

Scene 01 · Title pages

Everything about a title, then straight to the next episode

Seasons and episodes, cast, comments, videos and related titles in one place. The play button already knows where you are, so the next episode is one click away.

  • Mark watched now, on release day or on any date
  • Watchlist, favourites and your own Trakt lists
  • Trailers play inside the app
Scene 02 · Player

Jump to any episode without leaving the video

The player bar sits above the video, never over it. Pick a season, skip to an episode, switch servers or press Next. Pop-ups and in-player ads are blocked.

  • Opening a video sends nothing to Trakt
  • Preferred quality applied when the server allows
  • Recently played keeps your place on this PC
Scene 03 · Stats

Your stats and streak, no VIP needed

Filmie keeps a copy of your Trakt history on your PC and works out your totals, top genres, ratings and monthly activity from it. It's instant, and it works offline.

  • Movies, shows, episodes and time watched
  • A daily streak on Home
  • Deleted when you sign out

Features

Everything in one small app

Browse what's on

Trending, popular, anticipated, box office and the weekly charts, plus a calendar of what's coming out.

Sync with Trakt

Up next, continue watching, watchlist, lists, history and recommendations, kept in step in the background.

Marked only when you say

No check-ins and no scrobbling. Your history changes only when you press Mark watched.

Bring your own servers

Filmie ships with none. Add your own servers, then switch between them while you watch.

Fast and offline-ready

Pages come from a local cache first, so they open instantly and still work when the network doesn't.

Private by default

No accounts, analytics or telemetry of its own. Your data stays on your PC and with services you choose.

Get started

Up and running in minutes

  1. Install

    Download the installer and run it. It adds Microsoft's WebView2 runtime if your PC needs it.

  2. Connect Trakt

    Sign in with a short code to bring in your library. It's optional, so you can browse without an account.

  3. Add a server

    Filmie includes none. Add yours in Settings with its link and how its addresses are built.

FAQ

Questions

Does Filmie include movies or TV shows?

No. Filmie doesn't host or supply video and includes no video servers. It shows information from TMDB and Trakt and can open servers you add yourself.

Is it free?

Yes. Filmie is free and open source under the MIT License.

Do I need a Trakt account?

No, you can browse without one. You need a free Trakt account for your library, recommendations, stats and watch tracking. Trakt VIP isn't needed.

Why does Windows show a warning when I install it?

The installer isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may warn you. To continue, choose More info, then Run anyway. Each release lists a SHA-256 checksum you can verify first.

Does it work on Mac or Linux?

Not at the moment. Filmie runs on 64-bit Windows 10 and 11.

Are the screenshots real?

Yes. They're taken from the app itself, signed in to the developer's own Trakt account. The player shows a trailer, because Filmie ships with no video servers.
